model

These are prompting games. In each level an AI model has instructions it must follow, such as keeping a password secret or staying in character. You win by writing prompts that make it break those instructions.

Each level teaches one technique: role-play, few-shot examples, step-by-step reasoning or context injection. When you clear a level, the game shows which part of your prompt worked.
